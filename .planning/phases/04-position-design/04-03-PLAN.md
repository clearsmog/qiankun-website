Replace the site's positioning copy: a discipline-led homepage hero with one action into the work, a
finalized feature trio, a fact-only About page, a Contact page with a live email link, and site
metadata that describes a quantitative finance portfolio instead of a general technology blog.

in the rendered hero.

previewing blank.
