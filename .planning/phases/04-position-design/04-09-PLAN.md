<objective>
Bring the WorldQuant BRAIN and UK Finance Pay case studies up to the phase's positioning and exhibit
quality bar, and — on UK Finance Pay — perform the honest relabel: state plainly at the top that the
page is an illustrative data-storytelling exercise, remove the metric tile that dresses a panel count
as a finding, and strip any phrasing implying precision the figures do not have.

Purpose: WorldQuant BRAIN carries the only externally judged, independently verifiable credential on
the site and currently leads with its pipeline rather than the tier it reached. UK Finance Pay is the
weakest page on the site and its figures are hand-set while the Cisco page two clicks away claims
every number is computed and never typed. Two incompatible evidentiary standards under one roof is a
direct threat to the Core Value, and the fix CONTEXT.md locked is to relabel honestly — not to rebuild
and not to delete.

Output: two rewritten case studies carrying no colour literal, no unstamped exhibit, no unlabelled
axis, and — on UK Finance Pay — an honesty statement a reader meets before any claim.
</objective>
