<objective>
Make the projects index the page a hiring manager can scan in ten seconds: five cards in the locked
strength order, each leading with what the work produced, with the jargon translated and the academic
marks removed — and move the card styling out of the page and into the shared stylesheet so the site
stops running two card systems on adjacent pages.

Purpose: this page is the destination of the homepage's single hero action, so it is the second
surface a reader sees. Today its order is inherited from the sidebar rather than chosen, four of five
blurbs lead with method, and its styling lives in a page-local block that disagrees with the homepage
feature cards on radius, padding and spacing.

Output: one rewritten index page carrying no styling of its own, and one new section in `custom.css`
owning card presentation for the whole site.
</objective>
